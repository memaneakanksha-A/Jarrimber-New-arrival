At Jarrimber, we craft solid timber furniture by hand
in our Perth workshop using carefully selected
Australian hardwoods, including Western Australian
Marri and Jarrah, Tasmanian Blackwood and Oak, and
Victorian Ash and Blackbutt.

Timber is chosen for its grain, strength, character and
natural variation. The result is furniture shaped with
care and built to last.

Discover our story

Every home is different, which is why many of our
pieces begin with a conversation. We work with
clients to create furniture tailored to their space,
requirements and timber preferences.

From dining tables and entertainment units to beds,
vanities and one-off commissions, each piece is
designed and handcrafted in our Perth workshop.
